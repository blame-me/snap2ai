How it works

Three moves, one of them is a keystroke.

Nothing to configure on first run if you're already signed into an AI site. The default is Gemini, and you can change it from the toolbar in two clicks.

01

Press AltS

On the page that's confusing you. It captures the visible screen, not the whole scroll, so what you see is what gets asked.

02

Your AI takes it

A background tab opens, the image is attached, the prompt is sent. You don't touch any of that. The tab steps aside when it's done.

03

Read the green box

The short answer sits on top, the reasoning underneath, and one button copies it. Esc closes the panel and cancels anything still running.

It stays on your page

No tab hopping, no dragging screenshots into a chat window. The answer floats over the thing you were looking at.

It borrows your session

Snap2AI never asks for an API key. It uses the login you already have in that browser, so your plan and your history stay yours.

It keeps no diary

No analytics, no server of ours. Your screenshot goes to the AI you picked and nowhere else. Recent answers live in local storage.

Install

Four minutes, once.

It isn't on the Web Store yet, so this is the unpacked route. Same thing developers use, minus the sour face.

  1. 1

    Download and unzip

    Grab the zip from releases. You should end up with a folder called snap2ai.

  2. 2

    Open the extensions page

    brave://extensions in Brave, chrome://extensions in Chrome, edge://extensions in Edge. Turn on Developer mode, top right.

  3. 3

    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and pick the snap2ai folder itself, the one holding manifest.json. Pin the icon so you can see it.

  4. 4

    Bind the shortcut

    Go to brave://extensions/shortcuts, find "Screenshot the screen and get the answer", set it to Alt + S. If another app owns that combo, any other combo works.

When it misbehaves

Fixes, in order of how often they're needed.

Nine times out of ten it's the shortcut, a login wall, or a stale content script after an update. All three take under a minute.

Alt+S does nothing at all

Something else on the machine already owns that combo, usually a GPU overlay or a clipboard tool. Open brave://extensions/shortcuts and give Snap2AI another key. Also check the page: extensions can't run on chrome://, brave://, the Web Store, or the built-in PDF viewer.

It says you're not logged in

Sign into the AI site in this exact browser profile, then press Alt+S again. Guest windows, work profiles and containers don't share sessions. If your org uses SSO, finish that sign-in yourself first. Snap2AI can't fill credentials, by design.

It stalls on "verify you're human"

It won't click those for you, on purpose. Solve the check once in the AI tab, come back, and run the capture again. Once the site remembers you, the panel gets through without the detour.

The screenshot never attaches

Snap2AI tries the normal attach, then a drag-and-drop fallback, and retries twice. Keep the AI tab in the background and don't navigate it while the request runs. Other screenshot or clipboard extensions can steal focus mid-flow, so disable them for a test.

The panel spins and never answers

Press Esc to kill the request. Usually the AI tab landed on a consent screen or the service is having a bad day. Open the AI tab, clear whatever is blocking it, then retry. For a second opinion, set a Backup AI in Settings: if the first one fails, the same screenshot goes to the second automatically.

Nothing changed after you edited the files

Reload the extension: extensions page, circular arrow on the Snap2AI card. Then refresh the page you were testing. Old content scripts stay injected until both happen, which is why it can look like your change did nothing.

The answer is too long, or cut short

Settings holds the prompt. The default asks for one to three sentences plus the brief-reply line, which keeps replies tight. Delete that line if you want full explanations. Long answers get a "show more" in the panel.

It opened the wrong AI

Click the toolbar icon, pick the one you want, and that choice becomes the default. Settings has a separate Backup AI slot. If a site quietly changed its layout and the send button can't be found, Snap2AI waits about three seconds and then presses Enter instead.

Something we didn't cover? Open an issue and include your browser, the AI site, and what the panel said.
