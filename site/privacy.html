Privacy

Short version: your screen goes to your AI, not to us.

Last updated 18 November 2026

There is no Snap2AI server. The extension runs entirely in your browser. It has no analytics, no account system, no advertising and no way for the developer to see your screenshots or your answers.

What the extension handles

When you press Alt + S or click Capture, Snap2AI takes a PNG of the visible screen in the active tab and holds it in memory long enough to upload it to the AI site you selected.

It also stores the settings you choose (provider, prompt, toggles) and a short local history of recent answers.

Where the data goes

The screenshot is pasted into the AI web app you picked, in a background tab, using the session you are already signed into. From that point the image and the prompt are handled by that provider under its own privacy policy: Google Gemini, OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek or Mistral.

Snap2AI does not proxy, copy or retain the request. There is no intermediate server to leak.

What is stored on your machine

  • Settings in chrome.storage.sync, so they follow your browser profile.
  • Recent answers in chrome.storage.local, capped to a short list and clearable from the popup.

Uninstalling the extension removes both. Nothing is written to disk outside the browser's extension storage.

Permissions and why

  • activeTab captures the tab only after you trigger a capture.
  • tabs opens and focuses the AI page in the background.
  • scripting injects the answer panel and the automation helpers into that AI tab.
  • storage keeps settings and local history.
  • Host access is limited to the eight AI domains listed above.

What Snap2AI never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or fingerprinting.
  • No ads, affiliate links or data resale.
  • No reading of pages other than the ones you explicitly capture.
  • No collection of names, emails, payment details or location.

Your choices

Turn the extension off from the toolbar popup, clear history there, or uninstall it. You can also stay entirely signed out of the AI sites and never trigger a capture.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the diff is public in the site source.
