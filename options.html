How Blame made it

Snap2AI is a browser extension built by Blame. No API keys and no extra accounts — it works through the AI sites you're already logged in to.

  1. Press Alt + S and Snap2AI takes a screenshot of the page you're on.
  2. It opens your chosen AI (Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek or Mistral) in a tab, attaches the screenshot and sends your prompt.
  3. It waits for the AI to finish — ignoring "searching…" and thinking messages — then shows the final answer in a small panel on your page.
  4. If the AI fails, it automatically tries your backup AI.
